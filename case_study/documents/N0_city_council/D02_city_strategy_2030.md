# City: Strategy 2030

Issued by: Office of the Mayor, City Council
Adopted: March 2025

## Headline goals

| Goal | 2024 baseline | 2030 target |
|---|---|---|
| Annual bus trips | 28 million | 40 million |
| Car trips | 2024 level | 20% fewer |
| Road transport CO2 emissions | 2024 level | 35% lower |
| Water lost in the network | 22% | 12% |
| New affordable homes | not applicable | 5,000 |

## Principle
Each department reports upward in a summarized form. Detail stays with the unit that owns it.
