# Route 7 Pilot: Test Report

Author: Maryam Nazari, project lead
Date: 10 October 2025

## Setup
- Period: 4 August to 28 September 2025 (8 weeks).
- Route: Route 7. Six extra buses added from the existing fleet.

## Results

| Measure | Before | During pilot |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average peak-hour load | 94% | 71% |
| Average interval between buses at peak | 10 min | 6.5 min |
| Passenger satisfaction (out of 5) | 2.7 | 3.9 |

## Cost
Actual pilot cost: 298,000 EUR, against an estimate of 310,000 EUR.

## Status of the data check
The comparison between tap-in data and survey results (action from the 18 June meeting) is still pending.

## Recommendation
Extend the extra peak-hour buses permanently on Route 7. Route 12 was not piloted.
