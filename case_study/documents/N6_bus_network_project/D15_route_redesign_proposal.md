# Bus Network Redesign: Pilot Proposal

Project lead: Maryam Nazari, transport planner
Date: 16 June 2025

## Goal
Reduce peak-hour overcrowding so that no route exceeds the 85% load limit in the Transport Policy.

## Proposal
- Pilot on Route 7 for 8 weeks, starting 4 August 2025.
- Add 6 buses taken from the existing fleet.
- Shorten the peak-hour interval between buses from 10 minutes to 6 minutes.

## Why Route 7
Passengers name Route 7 as the most overcrowded (see the passenger survey), and it has the most complaints this quarter.

## Open question
The ridership report ranks Route 12 as the busiest route. The data must be checked before any permanent decision.
