Learn

The project in plain words

The idea in one minute

The problem

A city has departments, agencies and projects. Each one keeps its own documents. People ask questions, and the answer must come from the right documents, with a source.

The rule

A node answers only from its own documents (hard rule H01). The Transit Agency never answers from the Water Utility's files.

The method

RAG: find the 3 most relevant pieces of text, give them to a small language model, and ask it to answer with citations, or to say "insufficient knowledge".

The limit

Everything runs offline on a laptop with a 4 GB graphics card. So only small models fit, and we measure how good they are.

How we work

Four rules that keep the project safe

1. Your own folder

Write only in team/<your name>/. The lead copies finished work into the project.

2. Checks after every change

The lead runs tools/run_checks.py and the unit tests after copying work in. If something fails, it goes back to its author with the error.

3. One word, one meaning

Use the glossary words. A new word goes into the glossary first.

4. Never invent a fact

No made-up numbers, papers or product facts. Every claim about a tool has a link to its documentation.

The weekly meeting (30 min)

  • Each person: what I finished, what I am stuck on (3 minutes each).
  • The lead: what changed in the project this week.
  • Pick next week's steps.
